Racing 1

Each stage opens with a card listing what's on it. Press a pedal to start, then reach the finish before the clock runs out.

Two pedals

  • RIGHT pedal: gas. In the air it tips the nose down.
  • LEFT pedal: brake, then drive backwards. In the air it tips the nose up.
  • Use them in the air to land on your wheels. Landing on the roof is safe: the car is put back on its wheels after 1.5 seconds.

The clock, checkpoints and bonus time

  • The big number at the top is your time left. Each checkpoint you reach adds more.
  • Stunts add time too: a landed flip gives +2 s, a backflip +3 s, and a wheelie held for 1.5 s (on the rear or the front wheels) +2 s.
  • The bar under the clock shows how far you've come: a tick for each checkpoint (green once you pass it) and the finish on the right.

The buttons at the top

  • ? pauses the race and shows the race guide with this stage's tips.
  • II pauses: carry on, restart the stage, or go back to the garage.
  • RESET: stuck or upside down? It puts the car back a few metres.

Time's up

If the clock reaches zero you can watch an ad to CONTINUE +15 s, once per race. A finish after a Continue still counts for your stars and the leaderboard.

After the race

The results show your stars and what the race paid, line by line. Watch an ad for ×2 COINS, then race again with RETRY, go on with NEXT, or head back to the GARAGE.

The garage 3

Between races you're in the garage. Tap the ? at the top any time to see what every part of the screen does.

  • Top left: the world you're racing in. Tap it to open the STAGES window.
  • Top bar: the guide ?, settings (gear), leaderboard (trophy), fuel, your stars and your coins.
  • PARTS, CARS, TUNE: the three tabs, explained below.
  • Car panel: the car you drive, its Power (all its part levels added up) and what it's good at.
  • Bottom right: the stage picker and START RACE.
  • Bottom: the version and What's new, the notes for each update (with an UPDATE button when a newer version is on Google Play).

Upgrades: the PARTS tab 4

Six parts, each with its own levels. Upgrades belong to you, not to one car: every car you drive gets them. Tap UPGRADE to buy the next level; when you can't afford it yet, the button says how many coins you still need, and at the top level it says MAX. Tap a part card, or its ?, to see everything it does.

PartWhat it does
EngineTop speed, and more pulling power for ramps, ledges and mud. A faster car flies further off ramps and reaches the coins higher up. It also sounds deeper and louder.
GearboxAcceleration: how quickly the car gets up to its top speed, after every start, reset, crusher, wall or slow part. It doesn't raise the top speed.
SuspensionSofter, better damped springs, so the car settles after jumps and bumps. It also lifts the body (up to 25 cm at Level 10), so it gets stuck less on bumps, rocks and ledges.
TiresGrip: quicker starts and shorter stops, less sliding on ice, oil and snow, less slow-down in mud, sand, ash and deep snow, and no slipping back on steep ramps.
FrameAir spin: the car turns faster in the air, so more flips fit into one jump. A sturdier frame also keeps it steady on the road.
BrakesStronger braking before crushers, low bridges and ice (from Level 2 the front wheels brake too), and faster reversing.

Every stage shows the Power it's made for: green when your car has enough, amber when you're close, red when an upgrade or two would help.

Tune: the TUNE tab 6

Make the car you drive your own. Every car keeps its own settings. TUNE has two halves:

LOOK

  • Paint: the car's own colours, or fire red, hazard yellow, toxic green, ice blue, deep blue, grape, midnight or bone white.
  • Engine sound: the car's own engine, Rotary, Inline six, Rotary deep, Rotary screamer or Retro. Each tap plays it.

DRIVE

  • Ride height: how much of the Suspension's lift to use. Lower is steadier and harder to flip over, but bumps can catch the body.
  • Gearing: from a quick start to a higher top speed.
  • Grip: from more speed to more grip (better brakes, reversing and mud).

Stages and the STAGES window 7

  • Finish a stage to open the next one. Each world's stages get harder as you go.
  • Step through them with the < > arrows on the stage picker.
  • Or tap the stage name, the grid icon beside its stars, or the world name at the top left to open STAGES: every world along the top, and the world's stages as tiles over their own skies.
  • Each tile shows your stars, the Power the stage needs, your place on its leaderboard and your best time. A locked tile says which stage opens it. Tap a tile to pick that stage.
  • Worlds still to come show as COMING SOON.

Leaderboards 8

  • Every stage has its own leaderboard of the fastest finish times. Open it with the trophy in the garage's top bar, or from the results after a race.
  • It shows the ten fastest players, with their car, and your own place and best time.
  • Every finish counts, with or without a Continue. No internet? Your best time is kept and sent the next time you're online.
  • CHANGE NAME sets the name others see. Tap another player's name to hide it on your phone or report it.

Fuel 9

  • Your tank holds 10 cans, and every race uses one.
  • A new can comes every 20 minutes, up to 10.
  • Earn a new star and you get that race's can back.
  • Tap the fuel can in the top bar to see the wait, or watch an ad for 3 more cans (up to 10 ads a day).

Coins and stars 10

  • Every stage has 40 coins. Finish with 16 / 26 / 34 coins for 1 / 2 / 3 stars. Some coins hide high in the air, over jumps and vents.
  • Stars count only on a finish, and they unlock new cars.
  • A race pays for every coin you grab (coins are worth more in later worlds), for the distance you drove, a bonus for your first finish of a stage, and a bonus for each new star.
  • Spend coins on upgrades in the PARTS tab. Watch an ad after a race for ×2 coins.
