Racing 1

Each stage opens with a card listing what's on it. Press a pedal to start, then reach the finish before the clock runs out.

Two pedals

  • RIGHT pedal: gas. In the air it tips the nose down.
  • LEFT pedal: brake, then drive backwards. In the air it tips the nose up.
  • Use them in the air to land on your wheels. Keep the gas held over a ramp and the nose only tips gently for a moment; let go and press again to flip.
  • Landing on the nose or the roof is a crash and dents the car (see Damage). On its roof, the car is put back on its wheels after 1.5 seconds.

The clock, checkpoints and bonus time

  • The big number at the top is your time left. Each checkpoint you reach adds more.
  • Stunts add time too. In Worlds 1 and 2 a flip gives +2 s, a backflip +3 s, and a wheelie held for 1.5 s (on the rear or the front wheels) +2 s. From World 3 each one gives +1 s, so a better car matters more there.
  • A flip counts only when you land it on your wheels.
  • Some hazards take time away: a crusher's squash costs 4 s and a wrecking ball's hit 3 s (2 s each in Worlds 1 and 2).
  • The bar under the clock shows how far you've come: a tick for each checkpoint (green once you pass it) and the finish on the right.

The buttons at the top

  • ? pauses the race and shows the race guide with this stage's tips.
  • II pauses: carry on, restart the stage, or go back to the garage.
  • RESET: stuck or upside down? It puts the car back a few metres.

Time's up

If the clock reaches zero you can watch an ad to CONTINUE +15 s, once per race. A finish after a Continue still counts for your stars and the leaderboard.

After the race

The results show your stars and what the race paid, line by line. Watch an ad for ×2 COINS, then race again with RETRY, go on with NEXT, or head back to the GARAGE.

Ran out of time just short of a checkpoint or the finish, or got further than ever on that stage? Then RETRY is free: it uses no fuel.

Damage 2

Look after your car: crashes dent it, and a dented car is slower. The health bar at the top left shows how much is left, with the speed it costs underneath.

  • A crash is the body hitting the ground when the car isn't on its wheels: a nose or roof landing, a tumble, or the far edge of a gap. It costs 6 to 12% health, more for a harder hit. A light tap of the nose or the back doesn't count.
  • Landing on your wheels never damages the car, however hard.
  • Smaller knocks cost a little too: the belly slamming onto a bump, rock or ledge, driving head-on into something that stops you, a wrecking ball's hit, or a fall into a pit.
  • In Worlds 1 and 2 all damage counts half. A tougher Frame takes less (Level 10 halves it).
  • You can see it: the car looks battered under 70% health and wrecked under 35%, with smoke, then sparks.
  • Nothing repairs the car during a race, but every race starts with it as good as new.

Wrecked

When the health runs out, the race stops. Watch an ad to REPAIR 75% and drive on (once per race), or say No thanks and go to the results.

The garage 4

Between races you're in the garage. Tap the ? at the top any time to see what every part of the screen does.

  • Top left: the world you're racing in. Tap it to open the STAGES window.
  • Top bar: the guide ?, settings (gear), leaderboard (trophy), fuel, your stars and your coins.
  • PARTS, CARS, TUNE: the three tabs, explained below.
  • Car panel: the car you drive, its Power (all its part levels added up) and what it's good at.
  • Daily Delivery, under the car panel: a free crate of coins every day (see below).
  • Discord, bottom left: opens the Garage Rush Discord server, in the Discord app if you have it.
  • Bottom right: the stage picker and START RACE.
  • Bottom: the version and What's new, the notes for each update (with an UPDATE button when a newer version is on Google Play).

Daily Delivery

  • Once a day a crate of coins waits for you. Its crate bobs on the button while it's ready; once taken, the button shows how long until the next one.
  • It holds one race's pay in the furthest world you've opened, so it grows as you go.
  • Tap TAKE for the coins, or WATCH AD for 3 times as much.
  • A new crate comes every day at midnight UTC (02:00 in Romania, one hour later in summer).

Discord

Meet other racers, share your times and tell us what you think: join the Garage Rush Discord.

Upgrades: the PARTS tab 5

Six parts, each with its own levels. Upgrades belong to you, not to one car: every car you drive gets them. Tap UPGRADE to buy the next level; when you can't afford it yet, the button says how many coins you still need, and at the top level it says MAX. Tap a part card, or its ?, to see everything it does.

PartWhat it does
EngineTop speed, and more pulling power for ramps, ledges and mud. A faster car flies further off ramps and reaches the coins higher up. It also sounds deeper and louder.
GearboxAcceleration: how quickly the car gets up to its top speed, after every start, reset, crusher, wall or slow part. From Level 2 it drives the front wheel too, so good Tires let the car use all that pull. It doesn't raise the top speed.
SuspensionSoft landings: a hard landing costs speed, up to 20% at Level 1 and only 5% at Level 10. Better damped springs settle the car after jumps and bumps. It also lifts the body (up to 25 cm at Level 10), so it gets stuck less on bumps, rocks and ledges.
TiresGrip: quicker starts and shorter stops, less sliding on ice, oil and snow, less slow-down in mud, sand, ash and deep snow, and no slipping back on steep ramps.
FrameToughness: crashes, hits and falls dent the car less (Level 10 halves the damage). Air spin: the car turns faster in the air, so more flips fit into one jump. A sturdier frame also keeps it steady on the road.
BrakesStronger braking before crushers, low bridges and ice (from Level 2 the front wheels brake too), and faster reversing.

Every stage shows the Power it's made for: green when your car has enough, amber when you're close, red when an upgrade or two would help. From World 2 a stage also needs a minimum Power before you can start it (see Power to start).

Tune: the TUNE tab 7

Make the car you drive your own. Every car keeps its own paint, sound and settings. TUNE has two halves:

LOOK

  • Paint: the car's own colours, or fire red, hazard yellow, toxic green, ice blue, deep blue, grape, midnight or bone white.
  • Engine sound: the car's own engine, Rotary, Inline six, Rotary deep, Rotary screamer or Retro. Each tap plays it.
  • Driver: who sits behind the wheel. Eight racers, four boys and four girls, all free. You pick one when you first choose your name, and can change anytime here. Your driver drives every car.

DRIVE

  • Ride height: how much of the Suspension's lift to use. Lower is steadier and harder to flip over, but bumps can catch the body.
  • Gearing: from a quick start to a higher top speed.
  • Grip: from more speed to more grip (better brakes, reversing and mud).

Stages and the STAGES window 8

  • Finish a stage to open the next one. Each world's stages get harder as you go.
  • Step through them with the < > arrows on the stage picker.
  • Or tap the stage name, the grid icon beside its stars, or the world name at the top left to open STAGES: every world along the top, and the world's stages as tiles over their own skies.
  • Each tile shows your stars, the Power the stage is made for, your place on its leaderboard and your best time. A locked tile says which stage opens it. Tap a tile to pick that stage.
  • Worlds still to come show as COMING SOON.

Power to start

World 1 is for learning: any car can race it. From World 2 a stage needs a minimum Power before it can start, so you never get stuck at a jump your car can't make.

WorldPower needed to start
World 1None
World 270% of the Power the stage is made for
World 380%
Worlds 4 and 585%
  • From one stage to the next, the minimum never rises by more than 2. For example, Stage 31 needs Power 45, Stage 40 needs 59 and Stage 50 needs 76.
  • Too little Power? START RACE says NEED POWER and the number, and takes you to PARTS. The stage's tile in STAGES says Needs Power in red.
  • After a race, NEXT tells you when the next stage needs more Power, with an UPGRADE button that takes you to the garage.

Leaderboards 9

  • Every stage has its own leaderboard of the fastest finish times. Open it with the trophy in the garage's top bar, or from the results after a race.
  • It shows the ten fastest players, with their car, and your own place and best time.
  • Every finish counts, with or without a Continue. No internet? Your best time is kept and sent the next time you're online.
  • The first time you play you choose your racer name (the game suggests a free one, or type your own). Every name is unique: no two players share one.
  • CHANGE NAME sets a new name. Tap another player's name to hide it on your phone or report it.

Fuel 10

  • Your tank holds 10 cans, and every race uses one.
  • A new can comes every 20 minutes, up to 10.
  • Earn a new star and you get that race's can back.
  • Tap the fuel can in the top bar to see the wait, or watch an ad for 3 more cans (up to 10 ads a day).

Coins and stars 11

  • Every stage has 40 coins. Finish with 16 / 26 / 34 coins for 1 / 2 / 3 stars. Some coins hide high in the air, over jumps and vents.
  • Stars count only on a finish, and they unlock new cars.
  • A race pays for every coin you grab (coins are worth more in later worlds), for the distance you drove, a bonus for your first finish of a stage, and a bonus for each new star.
  • WORLD CLEARED! Your first finish of a world's last stage pays a big bonus on top.
  • Spend coins on upgrades in the PARTS tab. Watch an ad after a race for ×2 coins.
  • More coins every day: the Daily Delivery in the garage.
