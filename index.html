The game

Garage Rush is a side-scrolling stunt racer where the clock is always ticking.

Jump, flip and race across 50 hand-built stages in five worlds: dusty farm tracks, desert canyons, frozen peaks, a ruined city and an erupting volcano. Every stage mixes little hops, tall launch ramps and giant mega jumps with pits, ledges, ice, oil, ash, low bridges and smash barriers, plus slamming crushers, swinging wrecking balls and air vents that throw you skyward. Reach each checkpoint for more time, and land flips and wheelies for bonus seconds.

Build your car

Spend your coins in the garage on the engine, gearbox, suspension, tires, frame and brakes, 15 levels each. A stronger engine flies higher and reaches the coins hidden up in the air.

Five cars

The scrappy starter, the quick Sprinter, the heavy Hauler, the fast Interceptor and the grippy Ice Runner. Each one drives differently, and you can paint and tune it.

Think fast

Brake for the low bridge, then floor it for the gap. Watch the warning lights and slip under the crusher before it slams. Time your run past the wrecking ball. Every stage has its own big moment.

Collect stars

Grab coins for up to three stars per stage, unlock new worlds, and race for the fastest times on each stage's leaderboard.

Two pedals: easy to learn, hard to master.
